Step-by-Step Manual Testing Guide

Here is exactly how you can manually walk through and verify the entire end-to-end flow in the user interface, including custom approval flows, co-maker replacement, and returning a loan due to lacking requirements.

================================================================================
Phase 1: Configure Custom Loan Product & Approval Flow (New Feature)
================================================================================
1. Login as the Sako Admin:
   - Go to the login page.
   - Enter Company ID: 10001000 (or Email: admin@mlsako.com) and Password: password.
   - Click Sign In. You will land on the Admin dashboard.
2. Configure Custom Pipeline/Stages:
   - In the sidebar, click on "Loans Management".
   - Click "Edit Loan Requirements" (Edit icon) next to any product, or click "Create New Loan" to create a new one.
   - You will see a new section: "Custom Approval Flow Stages".
   - Check or uncheck stages to configure exactly which path this loan facility must undergo (e.g. check "Co-Makers", "Sako Staff", "HRMD Staff", "Credit Comm.", "Accounting", "Releasing Officer").
   - Click "Save Configuration".
   - JavaScript automatically preserves the correct logical chronological sequence regardless of the clicking order!

================================================================================
Phase 2: Apply for a Loan requiring Co-makers
================================================================================
1. Login as the borrower:
   - Go to your application login page.
   - Enter Company ID: 20248216 (or Email: member@mlsako.com) and Password: password.
   - Click Sign In. You will land on the member's dashboard.
2. Open the Loan Application:
   - In the sidebar, click on "Loans".
   - Pull out Accordion Drawer 1: Loan Package and select a package that requires co-makers, such as Petty Cash Loan with an amount of
     ₱5,000 (requires exactly 2 co-makers).
3. Select Co-makers:
   - Pull out Accordion Drawer 3: Cooperative Co-Makers.
   - Select exactly the number of required co-makers (e.g., select Jane Doe (Co-maker) and John Smith (Co-maker)).
4. Submit the Loan:
   - Enter member remarks in Drawer 4, read and accept the Terms Agreement.
   - Click "File & Submit Loan".
   - You will be redirected to "My Loans", and you'll see your application at the "Co-Makers" stage with status Pending.

================================================================================
Phase 3: Co-maker Rejection & Replacement (New Feature)
================================================================================
1. Login as Co-maker 1 (Jane Doe):
   - Sign out, and sign in with Company ID: 20248217 (or Email: jane.comaker@mlsako.com) and Password: password.
   - Click on "Co-Maker Requests" in the sidebar (marked with a red notification badge).
   - Click "Evaluate", enter remarks (e.g. "Sorry, I cannot co-sign at the moment") and click "Decline Endorsement".
2. Login as the borrower to Replace the Co-maker:
   - Sign out, and sign in back as the borrower (20248216 / password).
   - Go to "My Loans".
   - Under the Actions column, you will see a yellow "Replace Rejected Co-maker (Jane Doe)" button.
   - Click it, select a new co-maker from the dropdown (e.g. John Smith or another member), and click "Submit Replacement".
   - The old rejected record is archived, a new pending endorsement request is sent to the new co-maker, and the timeline reflects the change!

================================================================================
Phase 4: Sako Staff & HRMD Return Loan for Lacking Requirements (New Feature)
================================================================================
1. Endorse the Loan (John Smith approves):
   - Login as Co-maker 2 (John Smith): Company ID: 20248218 / password.
   - Go to "Co-Maker Requests", click "Evaluate", and click "Approve Endorsement".
   - The application advances to the "Sako Staff Review" stage.
2. Login as Sako Staff (Maria Santos) or HRMD (John Doe) to Return the Loan:
   - This corrective return feature is fully supported for BOTH the Sako Staff and HRMD Representative roles!
   - Sign out, and login as either:
     - Sako Staff: Maria Santos (Company ID: STAF0001 / password)
     - HRMD Rep: John Doe (Company ID: HRMD0001 / password) - Note: to test as John Doe, Maria Santos must first approve the loan to advance it to the HRMD stage.
   - Click "Loans" to view the review queue, and click the application row.
   - Under the Info Panel / Signatory actions, you will see three actions: "Sign & Approve", "Reject Application", and "Return Application" (New button!).
   - In Remarks, write what requirement is missing (e.g., "Lacking latest 2-month payslip document. Please upload or describe in remarks.").
   - Click "Return Application" and confirm. The application is successfully returned to the member!
3. Check the Email Notifications (New Feature!):
   - The borrower receives an automated, beautifully styled email notification: "Revision Required: Your Loan Application LN-[ID] Has Been Returned".
   - It utilizes a warm gold/amber color theme to indicate action required, showing the reviewer's custom remarks, loan product details, requested amount, and a direct CTA link back to the Member Portal.

================================================================================
Phase 5: Member Modifies & Resubmits (New Feature)
================================================================================
1. Login as Borrower to Resubmit:
   - Sign out, and login back as the borrower (20248216 / password).
   - Go to "My Loans".
   - You will see a yellow banner: "Returned (Correction Required)".
   - Click "Modify & Resubmit" (New button!).
   - You are taken straight to the Application Wizard, which is dynamically PRE-POPULATED with all your previous selections!
   - Correct the amount, change terms, or update remarks with the required information, and click "File & Submit Loan".
   - The application status resets back to "Pending" and goes back to the Sako Staff review queue, completely fresh!

================================================================================
Phase 6: Visual Timeline Tracker
================================================================================
1. View Timeline:
   - At any point on the member's dashboard, click "View Timeline" on any application.
   - A modern, graphical timeline tracking all transition states, endorsements, return remarks, and audit activities is displayed!
